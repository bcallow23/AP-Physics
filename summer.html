Pre‑Work: get your reasoning sharp

Welcome! Before our four summer sessions, spend a little time waking up the math and thinking skills we'll lean on. You don't need to learn any physics yet — just get comfortable moving symbols around and reading relationships.

Time: about 1–2 hours total Due: 8/18/2026 Focus: skills, not content

Why no physics content? Our summer sessions build the skills that make physics click: rearranging equations, reading graphs, designing experiments, and arguing from evidence. This pre-work simply makes sure your math toolkit is ready so day one feels smooth instead of rusty.

1

Math Readiness Tune‑Up

Required

Refresh these four skills. They show up constantly in physics, and being fluent with them is the single biggest predictor of a smooth start.

  • Rearranging and solving equations for any variable — e.g. solve \(v = v_0 + at\) for \(t\).
  • Exponents, powers, and scientific notation — e.g. \((2^3)^2\) and \(0.00042 = 4.2\times10^{-4}\).
  • Fractions and ratios without a calculator — adding, simplifying, and proportions.
  • Right‑triangle trigonometry — sine, cosine, tangent (SOH‑CAH‑TOA).
